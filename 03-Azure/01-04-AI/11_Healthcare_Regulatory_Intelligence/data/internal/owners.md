# Owners and internal due dates

Fictional training record. Owner: Regulatory Affairs Lead. Last reviewed: 2 January 2027.

Caldova uses roles, not personal names, in this register.

## Who owns which domain

| Domain | Owner role | Typical work |
|---|---|---|
| Labelling | Labelling Lead | Label text, translations and artwork: the content of a label change |
| Vigilance (pharmacovigilance and post-market surveillance) | Pharmacovigilance Lead | Safety reporting, PSURs, risk management plans, DHPCs |
| Clinical | Clinical Disclosure Lead | Trial registration and results disclosure |
| Manufacturing and quality | Quality Head | Sites, contract manufacturers, CAPAs, deviations, batch release |
| Market access | Market Access Lead | Payer dossiers, coverage, pricing schemes, forecasts |
| Regulatory submissions | Regulatory Affairs Lead | Preparing and submitting variations and supplements; contact with regulators |

## Approval

The **Regulatory Affairs Lead** approves the action plan for every new regulatory change. The **Quality Head** must also approve any action that affects product release or supply.

## How Caldova sets internal due dates

**Today** is the *Status as of* date at the top of `tracking/task-register.md`.

Apply the first rule that fits, using the deadline or date that belongs to **that action**:

1. If the document gives a deadline for the action, the internal due date is **14 calendar days before** that deadline.
2. If there is only an effective date, the internal due date is **30 calendar days before** the effective date.
3. If the document gives neither, the internal due date is **30 calendar days after today**. The approver may change it at sign-off.
4. If an internal due date is already in the past, flag the task as **at risk** and escalate to the Regulatory Affairs Lead.
