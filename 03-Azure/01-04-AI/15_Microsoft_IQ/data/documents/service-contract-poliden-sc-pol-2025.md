This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 Strandvik Drives AB, Gothenburg, Sweden ("Strandvik"), and Poliden AB, crusher site Skelleftehamn, Sweden ("Poliden").

1.2 Strandvik account owner: Sam, key account manager for Poliden. Poliden contact for planned stops: the Poliden maintenance planner, planner@poliden.example.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Strandvik services the drive units on crusher lines C1, C2 and C3 at Skelleftehamn. Crusher lines C1 and C3 run on SD-900 drive units. Crusher line C2 runs on an SD-700.

2.2 Annual contract value: 960,000 EUR.

## 3. Planned swaps

3.1 Poliden replaces worn drive units during planned maintenance stops.

3.2 Planned swaps are reserved at least 5 working days ahead. Strandvik reserves a unit of the right model in its ERP system (a reservation RSV-NNNN) and books a certified technician for the stop.

3.3 Poliden must confirm date changes in writing from its maintenance planner. A verbal remark, a forecast or a note from a Strandvik employee is not a confirmation.

3.4 When a stop date changes, Strandvik moves the reservation and the technician booking to the new date.

## 4. Unplanned failures

4.1 For unplanned failures Strandvik responds within 24 hours and delivers a replacement unit as fast as stock and transport allow. There is no uptime credit under this contract.

## 5. Requests

5.1 Poliden's registered agent may request quotes for parts and services. Orders need written approval from Poliden's maintenance planner. Strandvik follows its External Agent Access Policy EAP-01.

## 6. Contacts

Strandvik: Sam, sam@strandvik.example. Field service coordination: Noor, noor@strandvik.example. Poliden: maintenance planner, planner@poliden.example.
