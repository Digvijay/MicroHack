This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy tells Caldova Components AB how to share stock and components between customer orders when supply is short.

1.2 The goal is to protect the most important customer relationships and to make decisions fast and fair.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to finished products PV-400, CV-90 and BV-200.

2.2 It also applies to shared components. HX-220 is shared between PV-400 and CV-90. ST-50, SL-12 and FX-8 are shared between PV-400 and BV-200. FX-8 is also used in CV-90.

2.3 It applies to the Plant Manager, the Supply Planner and the Sales Lead, Key Accounts.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 Short supply: the stock and the inbound supply expected before the due date are not enough to build all open orders.

3.2 Customer tier: the tier of a customer in the customer master data. The tiers are strategic, key and standard.

3.3 Allocation: the decision about which open orders get the available stock or components first.

## 4. Allocation rules

4.1 When supply is short, allocate in this order:

1. Strategic customers.
2. Key customers.
3. Standard customers.
4. Within the same tier, by due date, earliest due date first.

4.2 Allocate finished goods on hand before components.

4.3 Allocate a shared component to the product that serves the highest tier order. For example, if HX-220 is short, HX-220 goes first to PV-400 for a strategic customer, then to CV-90 for a key customer.

4.4 Allocation must respect the Supplier Quality Policy SQP-04. Parts that SQP-04 blocks are not available for allocation.

## 5. Customer tiers (December 2026)

| Customer | Tier | Main product |
|---|---|---|
| Brennmark Industries GmbH | strategic | PV-400 |
| Solvik Marine AS | key | CV-90 |
| Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. | key | BV-200 |
| Østerdal Pumps AS | standard | PV-400 |

5.1 Brennmark Industries GmbH is the only strategic customer. When HX-220 is short, Brennmark Industries GmbH is served before Solvik Marine AS and Østerdal Pumps AS.

## 6. Override

6.1 The Plant Manager may override this allocation order in writing.

6.2 A written override must state the orders affected, the reason and the quantities.

6.3 The Plant Manager informs the Sales Lead, Key Accounts and the Supply Planner of every override.

## 7. Communication

7.1 After an allocation decision, the Sales Lead, Key Accounts informs every affected customer.

7.2 If a customer contract requires a notice, such as a force majeure notice within 48 hours, the notice must be sent on time.

## 8. Review

8.1 The Plant Manager reviews this policy once a year.
