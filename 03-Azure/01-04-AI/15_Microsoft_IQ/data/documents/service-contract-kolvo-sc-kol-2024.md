This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 Strandvik Drives AB, Gothenburg, Sweden ("Strandvik"), and Kolvo AB, cab plant Hisingen, Gothenburg, Sweden ("Kolvo").

1.2 Strandvik account owner: Alex, key account manager for Kolvo. Kolvo contact: Maria, production manager press shop.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Strandvik provides uptime service for the drive units at the Kolvo cab plant in Hisingen.

2.2 **Critical drive units**: the SD-900 drive units on press lines 1 to 3 (press line 1, press line 2 and press line 3).

2.3 Other covered units: the SD-700 on stamping line 1, with best effort response.

2.4 Annual contract value: 1,240,000 EUR.

## 3. Replacement target

3.1 Critical drive units (press lines 1 to 3) have a **replacement target of 48 hours** from the request.

3.2 The target is measured from Kolvo's request (by phone, email or Kolvo's registered agent) to the moment a replacement drive unit of the same model is delivered to the Kolvo plant, ready to install.

3.3 Strandvik installs. Installation is done by a Strandvik certified technician, at a time agreed with Kolvo's press shop.

## 4. Uptime credit

4.1 If a critical drive unit is down beyond the replacement target, Kolvo receives an uptime credit of 0.5% of the annual contract value per day of downtime beyond the target.

4.2 Downtime means the machine cannot run at all because of the drive unit. Running at reduced speed is not downtime.

## 5. Requests and orders

5.1 Kolvo's agent may request quotes on Maria's behalf. Orders need Maria's approval.

5.2 Strandvik follows its External Agent Access Policy EAP-01 for every request from Kolvo's agent. Prices and delivery dates are committed only by the Strandvik key account manager for Kolvo.

## 6. Priorities

6.1 Kolvo is a strategic account. Uptime of the press lines matters more to Kolvo than the price of a single delivery.

## 7. Contacts

Strandvik: Alex, alex@strandvik.example. Field service coordination: Noor, noor@strandvik.example. Kolvo: Maria, maria@kolvo.example.
