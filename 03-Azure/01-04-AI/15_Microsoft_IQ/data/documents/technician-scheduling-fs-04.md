## 1. Purpose

## 2. Slot types

| Slot | Hours | Notice needed |
|---|---|---|
| Day slot | 08:00 to 16:00 | 48 hours, or same day if a technician is free |
| Night slot | 22:00 to 06:00 | 24 hours |

2.1 A night install needs 24 hours' notice. The technician must rest before the shift.

2.2 Installation of an SD-900 takes 6 to 8 hours, so it fits in one slot.
