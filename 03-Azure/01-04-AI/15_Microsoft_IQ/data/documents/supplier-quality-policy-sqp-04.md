This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy protects the customers of Caldova Components AB from parts that may not be safe.

1.2 It explains when a supplier is placed on quality hold, what a quality hold means, and when a deviation may be granted.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This policy applies to every component that Caldova Components AB buys and uses in its products PV-400, CV-90 and BV-200.

2.2 It applies to all suppliers, including Nordhavn Castings A/S, Teglbruk Metall AS, Kvarnby Polymers AB, Rhein Präzision GmbH and Baltic Fasteners UAB.

2.3 It applies to all roles at Caldova Components AB, including the Supply Planner, the Plant Manager and the Sales Lead, Key Accounts.

## 3. Definitions

3.1 Quality hold: a status set by the Quality Head when a supplier delivers a lot that fails inspection or a test. In the supplier master data, the status is "quality_hold".

3.2 Deviation: a written approval to use a part that does not meet the normal rules. Only the Quality Head can approve a deviation.

3.3 Pressure-bearing part: a part that holds fluid under pressure in a finished valve. If a pressure-bearing part fails, the valve can burst or leak.

3.4 Corrective action: the actions a supplier takes to remove the cause of a failure. The Quality Head must approve the corrective action.

## 4. Pressure-bearing components

4.1 HX-220 is pressure-bearing. HX-220 is the valve housing used in PV-400 and CV-90.

4.2 SL-12, ST-50, FX-8 and AC-3 are not pressure-bearing.

## 5. Rules

5.1 No component from a supplier with status "quality hold" may be used without a written deviation approved by the Quality Head.

5.2 Deviations are not granted for pressure-bearing parts. This means that no HX-220 from a supplier on quality hold may be used, for any customer, under any time pressure.

5.3 A deviation for a part that is not pressure-bearing must state the part code, the lot numbers, the quantity, the reason and the end date.

5.4 Parts from a supplier on quality hold that are already in Borås are blocked in the warehouse system and may not be issued to production.

5.5 A quality hold ends only when the Quality Head approves the corrective action of the supplier in writing.

## 6. Approvals

| Decision | Who approves | Form |
|---|---|---|
| Place a supplier on quality hold | Quality Head | Quality report |
| Grant a deviation (not pressure-bearing parts only) | Quality Head | Written deviation |
| End a quality hold | Quality Head | Written approval of corrective action |
| Buy from a backup supplier | Supply Planner | Purchase order, after checking this policy |

6.1 The Plant Manager cannot override this policy. Allocation policy AP-02 does not change the rules in this policy.

## 7. Current quality holds

7.1 Teglbruk Metall AS is on quality hold. Lot L-7731 of HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS failed the pressure test on 4 December 2026. See quality report QR-2026-118.

7.2 Because HX-220 is pressure-bearing, no deviation can be granted. HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS may not be used until the quality hold ends.

## 8. Records

8.1 The Quality Head keeps all quality reports, deviations and corrective actions for 10 years.
