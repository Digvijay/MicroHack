This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The supplier is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The customer is Brennmark Industries GmbH, Hamburg, Germany.

1.3 Brennmark Industries GmbH is a strategic customer of Caldova Components AB.

## 2. Products

2.1 Brennmark Industries GmbH buys PV-400 pump valves from Caldova Components AB.

2.2 Brennmark Industries GmbH uses PV-400 in its compressor line in Hamburg.

2.3 PV-400 contains HX-220, SL-12, ST-50 and FX-8. The product catalogue of Caldova Components AB describes PV-400 in detail.

2.4 The price of PV-400 is 410 EUR per unit, delivered to Hamburg.

## 3. Orders

3.1 Brennmark Industries GmbH places purchase orders with Caldova Components AB. Each order states the quantity of PV-400 and the due date.

3.2 Caldova Components AB confirms each order in writing within 3 working days.

3.3 Only the Purchasing Manager of Brennmark Industries GmbH may change the quantity or due date of a confirmed order.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 Caldova Components AB delivers PV-400 to the Hamburg plant of Brennmark Industries GmbH on or before the due date.

4.2 Partial deliveries are allowed if the Purchasing Manager of Brennmark Industries GmbH agrees in writing. Without this written agreement, Caldova Components AB must deliver the full order quantity at once.

4.3 When a partial delivery is agreed, the written agreement must state the quantity and date of each part.

## 5. Late delivery

5.1 If Caldova Components AB delivers PV-400 after the due date, Caldova Components AB pays a late delivery penalty.

5.2 The penalty is 2% of the late value per week of delay. The late value is the value of the PV-400 units that are not delivered on the due date.

5.3 The penalty is capped at 10% of the late value.

5.4 Example: if 1,000 PV-400 worth 410,000 EUR are one week late, the penalty is 8,200 EUR.

5.5 No penalty applies to a delay caused by force majeure under clause 6, if the notice in clause 6.3 is given on time.

## 6. Force majeure

6.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by force majeure.

6.2 Force majeure means an event outside the reasonable control of the affected party. Force majeure includes port closures declared by a public authority, war, fire, flood and severe storms.

6.3 Caldova Components AB must notify Brennmark Industries GmbH in writing within 48 hours of becoming aware of a force majeure event. The notice goes to the Purchasing Manager of Brennmark Industries GmbH.

6.4 The notice must describe the event, the orders affected and a new expected delivery date.

6.5 If the notice is not given within 48 hours, Caldova Components AB cannot rely on force majeure for that delay, and clause 5 applies.

## 7. Communication

7.1 The Sales Lead, Key Accounts of Caldova Components AB is the main contact for Brennmark Industries GmbH.

7.2 Delivery promises to Brennmark Industries GmbH must be confirmed in writing by the Sales Lead.

## 8. Term

8.1 This agreement runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2027.

8.2 The parties will discuss renewal in March 2027.
