This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The buyer is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The supplier is Nordhavn Castings A/S, Denmark.

1.3 Nordhavn Castings A/S is the primary supplier of HX-220 to Caldova Components AB.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Nordhavn Castings A/S supplies HX-220 aluminium valve housings to Caldova Components AB.

2.2 HX-220 is used in PV-400 and CV-90. Caldova Components AB builds PV-400 pump valves and CV-90 control valves at its plant in Borås.

2.3 HX-220 is a pressure-bearing component. Every lot of HX-220 must pass a pressure test before it is shipped.

## 3. Orders and lead time

3.1 Caldova Components AB places purchase orders for HX-220 with Nordhavn Castings A/S. Each purchase order states the quantity, the price and the requested delivery date.

3.2 The standard lead time for HX-220 is 21 days from order confirmation to delivery in Borås.

3.3 Nordhavn Castings A/S confirms each purchase order in writing within 2 working days.

## 4. Shipping

4.1 Nordhavn Castings A/S ships HX-220 from the port of Esbjerg, Denmark.

4.2 Goods travel by sea and road from Esbjerg to Borås. Normal transit time is 2 days.

4.3 Nordhavn Castings A/S informs the Supply Planner of Caldova Components AB as soon as a shipment is delayed. This includes delays caused by weather or by a closure of the port of Esbjerg.

## 5. Price

5.1 The price of HX-220 is 38 EUR per unit, delivered to Borås.

5.2 The price is fixed until 31 December 2026. A change needs a written amendment signed by both parties.

## 6. Quality

6.1 Nordhavn Castings A/S delivers HX-220 that meets the drawing and the pressure test limits agreed with the Quality Head of Caldova Components AB.

6.2 Each lot carries a lot number and a test certificate.

6.3 Caldova Components AB may reject any lot that fails incoming inspection.

## 7. Backup supply

7.1 If Nordhavn Castings A/S cannot deliver HX-220 on time, Caldova Components AB may buy HX-220 from the backup supplier Teglbruk Metall AS.

7.2 Teglbruk Metall AS is the backup supplier for HX-220. The backup purchase must be notified to the Supply Planner of Caldova Components AB before the order is placed.

7.3 Any purchase from Teglbruk Metall AS is subject to the Supplier Quality Policy SQP-04. Under SQP-04, Caldova Components AB may not use HX-220 from a supplier that is on quality hold unless a written deviation is approved, and no deviation is granted for pressure-bearing parts.

7.4 A backup purchase does not change the obligations of Nordhavn Castings A/S under this agreement.

## 8. Force majeure

8.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control. This includes storms and port closures declared by a public authority.

8.2 Nordhavn Castings A/S must notify Caldova Components AB in writing within 48 hours of becoming aware of such an event, and give a new expected delivery date.

## 9. Term

9.1 This agreement runs from 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2028.

9.2 Either party may end the agreement with 6 months written notice.

## 10. Contacts

10.1 For Caldova Components AB: the Supply Planner for orders and logistics, and the Quality Head for quality matters.

10.2 For Nordhavn Castings A/S: the Key Account Manager and the Logistics Coordinator.
