# Frame Agreement FA-2025-009: Ferrovia Systems S.p.A.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document id | FA-2025-009 |
| Title | Frame Supply Agreement between Caldova Components AB and Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. |
| Owner role | Sales Lead, Key Accounts |
| Effective date | 2025-05-01 |
| Version | 1.1 |

This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The supplier is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The customer is Ferrovia Systems S.p.A., Verona, Italy.

1.3 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. is a key customer of Caldova Components AB.

## 2. Products

2.1 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. buys BV-200 ball valves from Caldova Components AB.

2.2 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. uses BV-200 in rail braking systems.

2.3 BV-200 contains ST-50, SL-12 and FX-8. BV-200 does not contain HX-220. Supply problems with HX-220 do not affect BV-200.

2.4 The price of BV-200 is 120 EUR per unit, delivered to Verona.

## 3. Orders and forecast

3.1 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. sends a rolling 6 month forecast for BV-200 every month.

3.2 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. places purchase orders at least 10 days before the due date.

3.3 Caldova Components AB confirms each order in writing within 3 working days.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 Caldova Components AB delivers BV-200 to Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. in Verona by road.

4.2 Partial deliveries are allowed if both parties agree by email.

4.3 Caldova Components AB keeps a safety stock of finished BV-200 in Borås equal to about 2 weeks of the Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. forecast.

## 5. Late delivery

5.1 If Caldova Components AB delivers BV-200 more than 5 working days after the due date, Caldova Components AB pays a penalty of 0.5% of the late value per week.

5.2 The penalty is capped at 5% of the late value.

## 6. Force majeure

6.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, including port closures declared by a public authority.

6.2 The affected party must notify the other party in writing within 72 hours of becoming aware of the event.

## 7. Quality and certification

7.1 BV-200 for Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. must meet the railway standards listed in the technical annex.

7.2 Caldova Components AB sends a certificate of conformity with each shipment.

7.3 Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. may audit the Borås plant once a year.

## 8. Communication

8.1 The Sales Lead, Key Accounts of Caldova Components AB is the main contact for Ferrovia Systems S.p.A.

8.2 The Supply Chain Manager of Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. is the main contact for Caldova Components AB.

## 9. Term

9.1 This agreement runs from 1 May 2025 to 30 April 2028.

9.2 Either party may end the agreement with 6 months written notice.
