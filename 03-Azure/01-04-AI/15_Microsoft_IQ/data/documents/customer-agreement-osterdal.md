# Standard Terms of Sale CA-2023-031: Østerdal Pumps AS

This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The supplier is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The customer is Østerdal Pumps AS, Oslo, Norway.

1.3 Østerdal Pumps AS is a standard customer of Caldova Components AB. These are the standard terms of sale of Caldova Components AB, with no special conditions.

## 2. Products

2.1 Østerdal Pumps AS buys PV-400 pump valves from Caldova Components AB.

2.2 Østerdal Pumps AS uses PV-400 in municipal water systems in Oslo.

2.3 PV-400 contains HX-220, SL-12, ST-50 and FX-8.

2.4 Prices follow the price list of Caldova Components AB. The current price of PV-400 is 410 EUR per unit.

## 3. Orders

3.1 Østerdal Pumps AS places purchase orders with Caldova Components AB by email or through the customer portal.

3.2 An order is binding when Caldova Components AB confirms it in writing.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 Caldova Components AB delivers PV-400 to Østerdal Pumps AS in Oslo by road.

4.2 Partial deliveries are allowed. Caldova Components AB may deliver an order in more than one shipment without asking Østerdal Pumps AS first. Caldova Components AB informs Østerdal Pumps AS of each shipment.

4.3 Delivery dates are best estimates. Caldova Components AB informs Østerdal Pumps AS as soon as it knows that a delivery will be late.

## 5. Late delivery

5.1 No penalty applies to late delivery under these standard terms.

5.2 If a delivery is more than 8 weeks late, Østerdal Pumps AS may cancel the late part of the order without cost.

## 6. Force majeure

6.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, including port closures declared by a public authority.

6.2 The affected party informs the other party without undue delay.

## 7. Payment

7.1 Payment is due 30 days from the invoice date.

7.2 Caldova Components AB invoices each shipment separately.

## 8. Warranty

8.1 Caldova Components AB gives a warranty of 24 months from delivery for PV-400.

8.2 Warranty claims go to the Quality Head of Caldova Components AB.

## 9. Communication

9.1 The Sales Lead, Key Accounts of Caldova Components AB is the main contact for Østerdal Pumps AS.

9.2 The Buyer of Østerdal Pumps AS is the main contact for Caldova Components AB.
