# Frame Agreement FA-2025-002: Solvik Marine AS

This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The supplier is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The customer is Solvik Marine AS, Bergen, Norway.

1.3 Solvik Marine AS is a key customer of Caldova Components AB.

## 2. Products

2.1 Solvik Marine AS buys CV-90 control valves from Caldova Components AB.

2.2 Solvik Marine AS uses CV-90 in ship ballast systems built in Bergen.

2.3 CV-90 contains HX-220, AC-3 and FX-8. The product catalogue of Caldova Components AB describes CV-90 in detail.

2.4 The price of CV-90 is 265 EUR per unit, delivered to Bergen.

## 3. Orders

3.1 Solvik Marine AS places purchase orders with Caldova Components AB. Each order states the quantity of CV-90 and the due date.

3.2 Caldova Components AB confirms each order in writing within 3 working days.

## 4. Delivery

4.1 Caldova Components AB delivers CV-90 to the Bergen yard of Solvik Marine AS on or before the due date.

4.2 No partial deliveries. Solvik Marine AS installs CV-90 per vessel section, and it cannot accept part of an order. Caldova Components AB must deliver the full order quantity in one shipment.

4.3 Early delivery is allowed up to 5 working days before the due date.

## 5. Late delivery

5.1 If Caldova Components AB delivers CV-90 after the due date, Caldova Components AB pays a late delivery penalty.

5.2 The penalty is 1% of the order value per week of delay.

5.3 The penalty is capped at 10% of the order value.

5.4 No penalty applies to a delay caused by force majeure under clause 6, if the notice in clause 6.3 is given on time.

## 6. Force majeure

6.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by force majeure.

6.2 Force majeure means an event outside the reasonable control of the affected party. It includes port closures declared by a public authority, war, fire, flood and severe storms.

6.3 Caldova Components AB must notify Solvik Marine AS in writing within 48 hours of becoming aware of a force majeure event. The notice goes to the Procurement Manager of Solvik Marine AS.

6.4 The notice must describe the event, the orders affected and a new expected delivery date.

## 7. Quality

7.1 Each CV-90 is pressure tested by Caldova Components AB before shipment.

7.2 Solvik Marine AS may inspect CV-90 at the Borås plant of Caldova Components AB with 5 days notice.

## 8. Communication

8.1 The Sales Lead, Key Accounts of Caldova Components AB is the main contact for Solvik Marine AS.

8.2 The Procurement Manager of Solvik Marine AS is the main contact for Caldova Components AB.

## 9. Term

9.1 This agreement runs from 15 January 2025 to 14 January 2028.

9.2 Either party may end the agreement with 6 months written notice.
