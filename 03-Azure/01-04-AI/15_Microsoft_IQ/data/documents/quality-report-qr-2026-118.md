## 1. Summary

1.1 Lot L-7731 of HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS failed the pressure test on 4 December 2026.

## 3. Test method

3.1 The Quality lab of Caldova Components AB tested a sample of 13 units from lot L-7731.

3.2 Each unit was held at a proof pressure of 24 bar for 60 seconds. The limit is no visible leak and a pressure drop of 0.2 bar or less.

4.1 Three of 13 samples leaked. The acceptance rule is zero failures in the sample. Lot L-7731 failed.

4.2 Cutting of sample 10 showed porosity in the casting wall near the outlet flange.

## 5. Decision

5.1 Lot L-7731 is blocked and will be returned to Teglbruk Metall AS.

5.2 Teglbruk Metall AS is placed on quality hold. The supplier status in the master data is set to "quality_hold".

5.3 No other lots of HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS may be ordered or used until the quality hold ends.

5.4 Earlier lots L-7310 and L-7522 from Teglbruk Metall AS passed inspection and were used in production. No customer action is needed for those lots.

## 6. Corrective action

6.1 Teglbruk Metall AS must send a root cause analysis and a corrective action plan for the casting porosity.

6.2 The expected date for the corrective action is 16 December 2026. The quality hold ends only after the Quality Head approves the corrective action in writing.

## 7. Impact

7.1 Teglbruk Metall AS is the backup supplier for HX-220. The primary supplier is Nordhavn Castings A/S.

7.2 While Teglbruk Metall AS is on quality hold, Caldova Components AB has no approved backup source for HX-220.

7.3 The Supply Planner must not plan any HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS.
