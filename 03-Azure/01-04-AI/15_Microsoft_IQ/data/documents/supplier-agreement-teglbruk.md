# Supplier Agreement SA-2025-019: Teglbruk Metall AS

This document is fictional and is used for training only.

## 1. Parties

1.1 The buyer is Caldova Components AB, Borås, Sweden.

1.2 The supplier is Teglbruk Metall AS, Norway.

## 2. Purpose

2.1 Teglbruk Metall AS is the backup supplier for HX-220. Nordhavn Castings A/S remains the primary supplier for HX-220 under agreement SA-2025-014.

2.2 Caldova Components AB uses this agreement only when Nordhavn Castings A/S cannot deliver HX-220 on time.

2.3 HX-220 is used in PV-400 and CV-90. HX-220 is a pressure-bearing component.

## 3. Orders and lead time

3.1 Caldova Components AB may place purchase orders for HX-220 with Teglbruk Metall AS at any time. There is no minimum yearly volume.

3.2 The standard lead time for HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS is 10 days from order confirmation to delivery in Borås.

3.3 Teglbruk Metall AS ships HX-220 from the port of Bergen, Norway.

3.4 The Supply Planner of Caldova Components AB must be notified before any order is placed under this agreement.

## 4. Price

4.1 The price of HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS is 44 EUR per unit, delivered to Borås.

4.2 An express surcharge of 5 EUR per unit applies if delivery is requested in less than 10 days.

## 5. Incoming inspection

5.1 Every lot of HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS needs incoming inspection at Caldova Components AB before use.

5.2 Incoming inspection includes a dimensional check and a pressure test on a sample from each lot. The Quality Head of Caldova Components AB sets the sample size.

5.3 A lot that fails incoming inspection is blocked and returned to Teglbruk Metall AS at the cost of Teglbruk Metall AS.

5.4 Incoming inspection normally takes 1 working day after the lot arrives in Borås.

## 6. Quality hold

6.1 If a lot from Teglbruk Metall AS fails incoming inspection, the Quality Head of Caldova Components AB may place Teglbruk Metall AS on quality hold.

6.2 While Teglbruk Metall AS is on quality hold, its parts are governed by the Supplier Quality Policy SQP-04. No HX-220 from Teglbruk Metall AS may be used during a quality hold, because HX-220 is pressure-bearing and SQP-04 does not allow deviations for pressure-bearing parts.

6.3 The quality hold ends only when the Quality Head approves a corrective action from Teglbruk Metall AS in writing.

## 7. Force majeure

7.1 Neither party is liable for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, including port closures declared by a public authority.

7.2 The affected party must notify the other party in writing within 48 hours of becoming aware of the event.

## 8. Term

8.1 This agreement runs from 1 June 2025 to 31 May 2027.

8.2 Either party may end the agreement with 3 months written notice.

## 9. Contacts

9.1 For Caldova Components AB: the Supply Planner for orders, and the Quality Head for inspection and quality holds.

9.2 For Teglbruk Metall AS: the Sales Manager and the Quality Manager.
