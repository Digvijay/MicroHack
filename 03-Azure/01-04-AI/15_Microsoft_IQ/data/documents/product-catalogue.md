## 1. About Caldova Components AB

1.1 Caldova Components AB makes industrial flow-control components in Borås, Sweden. Its customers are equipment makers in Northern Europe.

1.2 This catalogue always uses product codes and part codes. Please use the same codes in orders and messages, for example PV-400 and HX-220.

## 2. Products

| Product code | Name | Unit price (EUR) | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| PV-400 | Pump valve | 410 | Compressors and water pumps |
| CV-90 | Control valve | 265 | Ship ballast systems |
| BV-200 | Ball valve | 120 | Rail braking systems |

### 2.1 PV-400 pump valve

PV-400 is a pump valve for compressors and pumps. PV-400 contains HX-220, SL-12, ST-50 and FX-8, one of each. Brennmark Industries GmbH and Østerdal Pumps AS buy PV-400.

### 2.2 CV-90 control valve

CV-90 is a control valve with an electric actuator. CV-90 contains HX-220, AC-3 and FX-8, one of each. Solvik Marine AS buys CV-90.

### 2.3 BV-200 ball valve

BV-200 is a ball valve. BV-200 contains ST-50, SL-12 and FX-8, one of each. BV-200 does not contain HX-220. Ferrovia Systems S.p.A. buys BV-200.

## 4. Components

| Component code | Name | Primary supplier | Backup supplier | Pressure-bearing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HX-220 | Aluminium valve housing | Nordhavn Castings A/S | Teglbruk Metall AS | Yes |

4.1 HX-220 is used in PV-400 and CV-90. Nordhavn Castings A/S supplies HX-220 from Esbjerg, Denmark. Teglbruk Metall AS is the backup supplier for HX-220.

4.2 SL-12 is used in PV-400 and BV-200. Kvarnby Polymers AB supplies SL-12 from Malmö, Sweden.

4.3 ST-50 is used in PV-400 and BV-200. Rhein Präzision GmbH supplies ST-50 from Duisburg, Germany.

4.4 FX-8 is used in PV-400, CV-90 and BV-200. Baltic Fasteners UAB supplies FX-8 from Klaipėda, Lithuania.

4.5 AC-3 is used in CV-90. Rhein Präzision GmbH supplies AC-3 from Duisburg, Germany.

## 5. Production

5.1 The Borås plant can build 400 PV-400 per day. With approved weekend overtime it can build 600 PV-400 per day.

5.2 Every PV-400 and every CV-90 is pressure tested before shipment, because HX-220 is pressure-bearing.
