## 1. Purpose

1.1 Drive units are heavy (an SD-900 weighs about 4.2 tonnes on its transport frame) and must be moved fast when a customer machine is down. This document lists the standard routes, typical times and the approval rules for urgent transport.

## 2. Standard urgent routes

| Route | Mode | Typical time door to door | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rotterdam warehouse to Gothenburg | Truck and ferry (Kiel to Gothenburg) | about 25 hours | about 2,800 EUR |
| Pune factory to Gothenburg Landvetter | Air freight | 30 to 36 hours | about 40,000 EUR |
| Pune factory to northern Sweden | Regular consolidated air freight and truck | 6 to 8 days | about 9,800 EUR |
| Pune factory to Rotterdam | Sea freight | 23 to 26 days | about 3,500 EUR |

2.1 Rotterdam to Gothenburg by truck and ferry (Kiel to Gothenburg) takes about 25 hours. A truck that leaves Rotterdam at 08:00 is in Gothenburg at about 09:00 the next day.

2.2 Air freight from Pune to Gothenburg Landvetter takes 30 to 36 hours door to door. From Landvetter to the Hisingen area allow about 1.5 hours by truck.

## 3. Flight holds

3.1 A flight hold can be kept for 6 hours without cost. After that, the carrier charges the full freight.

3.2 A hold is not a booking. Holds may be placed by the Logistics team or the Logistics agent to keep an option open.

## 4. Approvals

4.1 Bookings above 10,000 EUR need KAM approval (the key account manager who owns the customer).

4.2 Bookings up to 10,000 EUR may be placed by the Logistics team once the KAM has approved the delivery plan.

4.3 Agents may find routes and times and hold a flight. They may not book without approval.
